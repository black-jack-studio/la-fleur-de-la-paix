## Scope

Whole site: home/showcase + interactive quote generator (wizard) + contact. Mode: Persuade.

## Audience, job, action, proof, constraints

Engaged couples / event organizers comparing florists mid-decision. Job: get a credible instant price sense before contacting. Action: complete the quote "record", then send it to the florist. Proof: transparent visible formula (composition × size), not a black box. Constraints: fictional content must read as plausible, not sourced; pinned palette (white/off-white + single rose-carmine accent) and pinned type pairing (readable script display + sober body) from PRODUCT.md brand commitments.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The quote is a cultivation record you fill in and stamp, not a web form — refuses the category default of a glossy photo hero over a generic "request a quote" card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Ivory ledger paper with a fine grid/ruled texture, carmine-rose ink as the single accent (headings, stamps, active states), circular ink-stamp motifs for confirmed choices, horizontal rule lines like a cultivation register, seed-packet-style bordered labels for each flower variety, script face for the wordmark and variety titles, a sober serif/grotesk for body copy, small tracked uppercase mono-ish labels for data/price line-items (raised from the Design Annual Plate Section challenger: hairline rules + tracked mono labels give the price breakdown ledger rigor instead of soft card UI).

STORY: Visitor lands on a "specimen register" for the maison; understands this is a real florist with real craft (rose "La Paix" as the signature variety, name-checked); moves into the quote wizard as if opening a page of the register; each choice (occasion/composition type, size) fills a labeled field; the total appears as a stamped, itemized ledger line; visitor then sends that stamped record to the florist via a contact step.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-width ivory register page. Large script wordmark "La Fleur de la Paix" top-left or centered, a small circular stamp/seal near it. Below: a "fiche" introducing the maison and the rose La Paix in a labeled specimen-card layout (image plate + Latin/common name label + short note), with the primary action "Ouvrir mon carnet" styled as an ink stamp button, positioned where the eye lands after the register intro.

FORM: Journal du jardinier-rosiériste (gardener/rose-grower's cultivation ledger), rank 7 of 7 on my resonance-ordered grounded list, seed key a544b0b8. Raises: (1) from Design Annual Plate Section (declined, competitive on product clarity) — tracked-uppercase ledger line-items with hairline rules for the price breakdown; (2) from Signals/Instruments Darkroom Safelight Bay (declined) — wizard steps commit irreversibly like a development stage, stamped rather than left as an editable floating field.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
